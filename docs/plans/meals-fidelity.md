# Meals to the artboard

Superseded for styling on 2026-09-19 by the Holon redesign (docs/plans/holon-product-redesign.md, section 4): colour, type, radii, surfaces and control shapes now come from the Holon design contract and the shared components in `components/pos/`. This plan still governs what each screen shows and how it is laid out.


## Context

Screen seven of the fidelity pass (method: docs/plans/weekly-review-fidelity.md). `POS Meals.dc.html` captured at 1440x900 (the week page also at 977 tall) into `/private/tmp/pos-handoff-sources/meals/`: Week, last week, Recipes, Recipes filtered to favorites, the Pick drawer, the Grocery drawer, the recipe drawer opened from a planned cell and from the library (one with a source link, one with a note), dark and light, 20 PNGs. The app has the right data (recipes with per-serving macros, cost and tags; one entry per slot per day with `eaten`; drafts from an agent write; cook mode) and the wrong screen: four tabs (Week, Recipes, Grocery, Inbox) where the artboard has two and two drawers; a rolling seven days from today laid out as day rows and slot columns where the artboard is a Monday to Sunday grid of slot rows and day columns; four metric tiles (Planned, Eaten, Protein, Unknown) where the artboard has a Today strip with four macro bars and four week cells (kcal a day, protein a day, cost, cooked); a recipe list with an inline card where the artboard has a card grid with tag chips and a 520px drawer; no favourite toggle, no pick drawer, no way to remove a meal from the screen, no cost anywhere, no way to get a recipe in from the UI. What the app has that the artboard does not: cook mode (SPEC, built), the draft inbox (an imported recipe waits), the "unknown" count for meals eaten with no recipe, the calorie estimate sentence, the by-item grocery merge.

Decisions with Nick (append to `decisions/log.md`):
- The two tabs are Week and Recipes; the Grocery tab becomes the drawer behind the "Grocery list" ghost button and the Inbox tab goes: drafts are cards at the top of the Recipes grid with an amber DRAFT tag and Accept / Discard minis in place of the usage line, and the drawer on a draft has Accept / Discard in its footer. The existing inbox test moves to `?tab=recipes`.
- The week is Monday to Sunday (artboard), paged with the stepper, `?week=` in the URL as today. The page loads four weeks either side of today; further paging shows an empty grid that "+" still plans into.
- "Paste a recipe URL to ingest" is built: a new `import_recipe` tool fetches the page, reads the schema.org `Recipe` JSON-LD (name, ingredients, steps, yield, total time, nutrition per serving when present) and inserts it as a `draft`, no model call, no dependency, and a page with no Recipe JSON-LD says so rather than guessing (SPEC section 4 amended: no `recipe-scrapers` fallback, it is Python and cannot run on Vercel). Alternative: left out, in which case the UI still has no way to add a recipe.
- "Suggest week →" is built as a deterministic `fill_week` tool: every empty slot from today to Sunday gets a ready recipe tagged with that slot, favourites first, rotating by day and slot; a slot with no tagged recipe stays empty; no model, and the copy claims none. Alternative: left out.
- Targets: the only target the app has is the calorie estimate from Fitness (fifteen calories a pound of body weight). Protein, carbs and fat have no target anywhere, so their bars and "/ target" suffixes are not drawn, the protein figures take no colour, the band dot reads the kcal standing, and "target from Fitness" appears only on the kcal cell, as "15 kcal a pound, from Fitness". A protein target is a Fitness decision, not a Meals one.
- Not drawn because the app has no source: the XP number ("+15 XP", "+240 XP": the weight is 6 an event and lands only on linked skills, so the amount is not known on screen), the "Nutrition skill" link, the "Groceries budget" link to Finance, the "Vault note" block and its Second Brain link (a recipe's `notes` is text and links to nothing; it is shown as a Notes block when non-empty), the "Pantry staples are left out" sentence (the app has no pantry), and drag and drop between cells (Swap and Remove from plan in the drawer cover a move, as the Tasks pass decided for its calendar).
- The ad hoc "Ate something else?" field is kept as a label-only log: it puts the text into the first empty slot of today as eaten (the `plan` tool's label path), counted as "without a recipe" in the strip; the kcal and protein parsing is not built because `plan_entry` has no macro columns and adding them is a migration. Placeholder: "Ate something else? e.g. burrito". Hidden when today has no empty slot. Alternative: left out.
- The grocery drawer groups by recipe as drawn ("3× · serves 4", quantities as written, "× n" when a serves-one recipe is planned n times); the by-item merge (`groceryList()`) and its tests are retired and the README paragraph reworded. Ticks are component state and clear on reload (persisting them is a table). Meta reads "N items · M checked · est. $cost" where cost is the sum of the planned recipes' cost, which is what the owner typed, not a store price.
- Cook mode stays, entered from a "Cook" ghost in the recipe drawer footer (both states) rather than from the old card. Nothing else on the artboard is dropped for it.
- Cost on screen is `cost_cents / servings` a serving times the entry's servings; the recipe card's "/serving" and the drawer's "Time · cost" cell are per serving; day and week totals sum entries.
- The lede is the artboard's with two claims made true: "Plan the week, log what you ate, and see calories against the Fitness estimate. Cooking a planned meal earns XP on the skills the recipe is linked to."

## Files

- `modules/meals/manifest.ts`: tools `import_recipe` (zod `{ url: url }`; fetch, `parseRecipe()`, insert as `draft`, register), `fill_week` (zod `{ from: date, to: date }`; the pure picker over ready recipes and the week's empty slots, one `plan` insert each), and `write_recipe` as it is for the favourite toggle. Nothing guarded (nothing spends).
- `modules/meals/jsonld.ts` (new, no imports): `parseRecipe(html)` reads every `<script type="application/ld+json">`, finds `@type` Recipe (also inside `@graph`), returns name, ingredients (`recipeIngredient` split into quantity and item on the first word that is not a number or unit, or the whole string as the item), steps (`recipeInstructions` strings or HowToStep `text`, flattened through HowToSection), servings (first integer in `recipeYield`), `time_minutes` from `totalTime` ISO 8601, kcal/protein/carbs/fat from `nutrition` (leading number of `calories`, `proteinContent`, `carbohydrateContent`, `fatContent`; treated as per serving as schema.org defines them; absent is 0). `jsonld.test.ts` first.
- `modules/meals/fill.ts` (new, no imports): `fillWeek(emptySlots, recipes)` returns `{ on_date, slot, recipe_id }[]`; `fill.test.ts` first (favourites first, rotation, an untagged slot stays empty).
- `modules/meals/macros.ts`: `costCents(recipe, servings)` per serving times servings; `groceryList()` retired with its tests; a `groupByRecipe()` for the drawer if it is more than a reduce in the component (it is not: do it inline).
- `modules/meals/data.ts`: the page window (`listPlan(monday - 28 days, 63)`); `listPlan` unchanged for the digest.
- `modules/meals/ui/actions.ts`: `toggleFavourite`, `fillWeek`, `importRecipe`, `removeFromPlan` (the `plan` tool with no recipe), `logAdhoc` (the `plan` tool with a label, then `mark_eaten`).
- `modules/meals/ui/MealsPage.tsx`: PageHeader with the band summary as `status`, the lede, `actions` (Grocery list ghost, Suggest week); passes the window and the target.
- `modules/meals/ui/Meals.tsx`: tabs, stepper, Today strip, the grid, the week cells, the Recipes filter row, ingest form and card grid; drawer state in the URL. `modules/meals/ui/Drawers.tsx` (new): Pick, Grocery and Recipe drawers on the shared Overlay. Cook mode stays in `Meals.tsx` as it is.
- `modules/meals/seed.ts`: the demo week planned Monday to Sunday of the current week (compute Monday from `select core.today()` in the seed), days before today eaten with one gap, two entries next week; `notes` on Turkey chili so the Notes block has something to show. `e2e/seed.mts` calls it and is not edited.
- `modules/meals/README.md`: the grocery paragraph reworded (grouped by recipe, quantities as written), a paragraph on import (JSON-LD only, drafts) and on suggest (no model).
- `docs/SPEC.md` section 4: the amendment on the fallback.
- `e2e/screens.spec.ts`: the four meals tests reworked (below).
- Constraints: no edits to `components/pos/*`, `core/*`, `app/*`, `e2e/seed.mts`, `supabase/migrations`. The Overlay, TabBar (`tabClassName`, `countTone`) and PageHeader already carry what this screen needs.
