# Insurance to the artboard

## Context

Screen seven of the fidelity pass (method: docs/plans/weekly-review-fidelity.md). `POS Insurance.dc.html` captured at 1440x900 into `/private/tmp/pos-handoff-sources/insurance/` (page, the policy drawer in view, revealed, auto and life, edit, new, and the upload drawer idle, running and review; dark and light, 17 PNGs). The app already has the right bones: the same band, title block, four-tile strip, the expiry-sorted table with masked numbers, a view drawer with a separate reveal action, an edit drawer, an upload drawer that drafts fields from a PDF through Haiku, `write_policy` and `renew_policy` guarded, and drawer state in the URL. What differs: the band's right-hand text (the app writes the expiring count as the crumb; the artboard's crumb is "Insurance / Policies" with a "{n} policies · {m} expiring soon" summary and a coloured dot on the right), the lede, the primary button (DS 51px ink button with an arrow, the app draws a brand outline), the tile sub-lines (11px sentence case, the app's are 10px uppercase), the table (the artboard has five columns with the kind inside the Policy cell, "37 days" over "Oct 18", "/mo" cadence tags, "60d · 14d" reminders, a 9px 88px status pill in the expiry colour), and the whole drawer: the artboard draws a kind, name, carrier and status head, a bordered number button with REVEAL inside it, a three-cell strip (Expires, Premium, Deductible with the limits under it), a Payment schedule card, a Renewal reminders card with a timeline, a Documents list with "+ Attach PDF", an Agent card, and a footer with Edit, "Mark renewed · {date}" and Delete. The edit drawer is a 2 by 2 form with a reminders and Finance card and Cancel / Save → in the footer; the app's is a single column with pill groups. The upload drawer has a drop zone, a running card with steps, and a review grid with a confidence column. The app has three things the artboard lacks: an `undated` and a lapsed or cancelled state, a quarterly cadence and a longer kind list (a check constraint), and two agent columns. The artboard has four things the app cannot honestly draw as written: "posting to Finance" (nothing in Finance reads `post_to_finance`), a Payment schedule (no payments or billing dates exist), an upload flow that "extracts text locally" in three steps (the app makes one model call), and Delete and "+ Attach PDF" (no tool and no action yet). The artboard draws no adequacy or "underinsured" judgment anywhere, so the SPEC section 8 rule is not at risk on this screen; the only judgment copy on the screen today is the app's own disclaimer, which the artboard does not have.

Decisions with Nick (append to `decisions/log.md`):
- `post_to_finance` is a flag nothing in Finance reads, so "posting to Finance" and "Shows as a recurring charge on the Finance timeline" are not true. Default: the switch stays as recorded intent and the copy says so: the tile reads "{n} marked for Finance", the payment card's label reads "MARKED FOR FINANCE" / "NOT MARKED", the switch's helper reads "Recorded on the policy. Finance does not draw it yet.", and the lede's clause "premiums can post into Finance as recurring charges" is left out. Building the link (a `finance.recurring` row from an insurance flag) is a cross-schema write for a later pass.
- Payment schedule: the app stores no payments and no billing day. Default: draw the card with dates stepped back from the expiry by the cadence (a monthly policy expiring Oct 18 bills on the 18th), rows "Last · Next · Upcoming · Upcoming" with the premium, and never "Paid" (no payment record exists). Alternative: leave the card out.
- Upload flow: the artboard says "Text is extracted locally" and lists three steps; the app sends the PDF to Anthropic in one Haiku call. Default: the drop zone with the true sentence ("Declarations page works best. The PDF goes to Anthropic with your own key and the fields are drafted for you to confirm. Nothing is saved until you do."), a running card with the file name and one step "Drafting fields · Haiku", the review grid as drawn.
- Delete: a new guarded `delete_policy` tool (asks first with `confirm()`), removing the row, its documents (cascade) and their files from the bucket, like Travel's `delete_trip`.
- "+ Attach PDF" and document links: a new `attach_document` action (upload to the module's private bucket, a document row with meta "{size} KB · {Mon YYYY}"); a document with a file opens through a short-lived signed URL, a file-less demo row is plain text. Page counts are not known (no PDF parser), so "{n} pages" is written only where the seed already says it.
- The adequacy disclaimer: the app's lede sentence ("What each policy covers is recorded ... no gap analysis, no scoring, no opinion") and the drawer paragraph ("Nothing here scores it ...") are the app's own; the artboard has neither and draws no judgment. Default: drop both; the rule holds by construction (README, SPEC 8) and the e2e assert on that paragraph goes. The lede becomes "Sorted by what expires first. Reminders fire 60, 30 and 7 days out by default." (the app's DB default is 60, 30, 7, not the artboard's 60).
- "Push + email, per your Settings › Notifications": read the `policy_renewal` rule's channels from `core.notification_rules` (it exists: `{push,email,inapp}`) and name them; "Reminders are off in Notifications." when the rule is muted. Same as the Tasks reminder channel cell.
- Agent: the app stores `agent_name` and `agent_contact`; the artboard has one "Agent · contact" field split on " · ". Default: one full-width row labelled "Agent · contact" holding two inputs (name, contact), so nothing is parsed out of a middle dot.
- Cadence and kinds: the app has quarterly and eleven kinds (a check constraint; the artboard has three cadences and eight kinds with "Phone" for the app's "device"). Default: keep the app's lists; the row's cadence tag is "/mo", "/3mo", "/6mo", "/yr"; the kind label is the enum uppercased.
- The app's own states the artboard lacks: `undated` (no expiry on file) and lapsed or cancelled. Default: keep; pill "NO DATE", "LAPSED", "CANCELLED" in the quiet tone, the days cell reads "no date", a lapsed or cancelled name is ink-3. The band count and the Annual premium tile count active policies only, as today.
