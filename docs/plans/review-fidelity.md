# Review to the artboard

## Context

Core screen of the fidelity pass (method: docs/plans/weekly-review-fidelity.md). `POS Review.dc.html` captured at 1440x900 into `/private/tmp/pos-handoff-sources/review/` (pending with the first card selected, a guarded card selected, the After cell in edit, the Approved tab, the Dismissed tab, and the empty inbox; dark and light, 12 PNGs). The app already has the right bones: the same band, title and lede, the tab in the URL (`?tab=`), the two-pane list with a sticky detail panel, Approve, Dismiss, Undo and "Approve all non-guarded", inline edits carried as a `patch` that `approve()` re-validates through the tool's own schema, and a toast per decision. What differs: the band's right-hand text (the app puts the dot on the crumb and "{n} pending" beside the bulk button; the artboard's crumb is "Review / Pending" with a plain slash and the dotted "{n} pending" sits at the band's right), the tabs (TabLinks at 10px side padding, the artboard's are 16), the list grid (the app flex-wraps 420/320 with a 24px gap; the artboard is `1fr 1.1fr` gap 14), the card (the app draws an uppercase eyebrow, three bordered chips and a StatusChip; the artboard draws an 11px sentence-case "from · when" line, plain 10px mono state words, a 14px title and an 11px meta row with the module, the tool and "{n}% confident" in the confidence colour), the relative time ("1m ago" against "04:02 today"), and the whole detail panel: the artboard has the state word top right, a Current / After two-cell grid (the app draws the field | before to after DiffList), a three-cell strip for Confidence, Evidence and Affects (the app stacks them as sections), an Edit that turns the After cell into an input with "Save & approve" and "Cancel edit", a Dismiss pushed to the right, a 12px amber guarded line, and a decided line with Undo. The app has two things the artboard lacks: proposals with no confidence and no diff (the fitness coach's), and a fourth stored status `rejected` with no tab. The artboard has four things the app cannot honestly draw as written: "sets is_manual = true" and "the row is marked manual" (approve calls the module tool with `source: 'agent'`; only some tools set `is_manual`), "The agent will not re-propose this for 30 days" (a dismissal takes the row out of the inbox; nothing in `core/tools.ts` reads `dismissed_until` before proposing again), Undo on an approved proposal (`reopen()` puts the row back to pending and the write stays; the Agent Log entry approve writes carries no revert payload), and "the nightly run at 04:00" (the cron is `0 9 * * *` UTC, which is 04:00 only in Chicago summer time). Nothing on the screen claims a proposal was applied without a decision or that the app judges which to approve, and none is planned.

Decisions with Nick (append to `decisions/log.md`):
- "sets is_manual = true" (guarded line) and "the row is marked manual" (decided line) are not true of every tool: `approve()` runs the module's tool with `source: 'agent'` and only Finance, Health and Skills tools touch `is_manual`. Default: the guarded line reads "Guarded tool: approving writes to {Module} immediately." and the decided line "Approved. {module.tool} ran."
- Undo on an approved proposal: `core.reopen()` moves the row back to pending without reversing the write, and approving again would run the tool a second time. Default: Undo only on the Dismissed tab ("Back in the inbox"); the Approved tab shows the decided line and no button. Alternative: keep Undo on both and change the copy to say the write stays (the reversal itself lives in the Agent Log and needs a revert payload that `approve()` does not log; a `core/*` change, Blocked).
- "Dismissed. The agent will not re-propose this for 30 days." is not enforced: `dismiss()` writes `dismissed_until`, but the pending list already excludes the row by status and `core/tools.ts` never reads the window before inserting a new proposal (the fitness coach has its own pending-only repeat guard). Default: the decided line reads "Dismissed. Undo puts it back in the inbox." and the toast reads "Dismissed". Alternative: make `core/tools.ts` honour `dismissed_until` for the same module, tool and title (core, Blocked) and keep the 30-day sentence.
- Empty inbox copy "Next proposals arrive after the nightly run at 04:00.": the hour is the Vercel cron `0 9 * * *` UTC in the owner's zone. Default: compute it with `clockIn(new Date(Date.UTC(y, m, d, 9)), settings.timezone)` from `core/today` so the sentence reads "at 04:00" in Chicago summer and "at 03:00" in winter. Alternative: "after the nightly run." with no hour.
- The Approve button: the artboard's source asks for a small DS primary (13px, `padding 9px 14px`, 120 by 38, logic line 158) but the rendered artboard ignores that override and draws the full DS `.btn` at 51px (15px/500, `padding 14px 22px`) with the arrow, and Edit and Dismiss stretch to 51 beside it. Default: what the artboard shows, `ActionButton variant="solid" size="xl"` "Approve →" / "Save & approve →" with Edit and Dismiss stretched to the row. Alternative: the source's intent, `lg`-like 38px.
- Proposed change with more than one diff entry: the artboard has one Current / After pair per proposal; the app's `diff` is a list. Default: one row of two cells per entry, labelled "Current" and "After" when there is a single entry and "{field} · current" / "{field} · after" otherwise. A proposal with an empty diff (the coach's) draws no Proposed change block and no Edit button, as today.
- Edit only where it is honest: the patch key is the diff entry's `field`, and `approve()` merges it over the payload and re-parses, so an entry whose `field` is not a payload key would be dropped silently by the schema. Default: page.tsx passes `editable` per entry (`field in payload`); Edit appears when at least one entry is editable and only those cells become inputs.
- Relative time: the artboard writes "04:02 today", "yesterday", "2 days ago"; the app writes "1m ago", "3h ago", "2d ago". Default: the artboard's, in the owner's zone (`clockIn` and `dayIn` from `core/today`, `getSettings().timezone`).
- Null confidence, evidence or affects (the coach's proposal has none): the artboard's data always has all three. Default: the card omits the "confident" span; the strip's cell reads "not given" in ink-4.
- "Approve all non-guarded ({n})": the artboard shows it whenever anything is pending, on every tab, and would show "(0)". Default: on every tab, only when at least one unguarded proposal is pending.
- Decided cards at 75% opacity (logic line 141): the app's 2026-09-08 rule is ink, not opacity. Default: title ink-2 and the meta row ink-4 on a decided card.
- The tab row: the shared `TabLinks` has no `tabClassName`, so its side padding stays 10px against the artboard's 16. Default: a client `ReviewTabs` wrapper around the DS `TabBar` (which has `tabClassName`) that pushes `?tab=` through the router, keeping the tab in the URL. Alternative: add `tabClassName` to `TabLinks` (components/pos, Blocked). The DS tab's 8px top padding against the artboard's 10 is not chased.
- Keyboard shortcuts J/K/A/D are benched in the artboard (logic line 113) and are not built.
- Selection in the URL: `?sel=<id>` written with `history.replaceState` so the guarded-card state survives the e2e reloads and the side-by-side shows it; the first visible card is the default, as on the artboard.
