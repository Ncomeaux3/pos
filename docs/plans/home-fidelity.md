# Home to the artboard

## Context

Screen eight of the fidelity pass (method: docs/plans/weekly-review-fidelity.md). `POS Home.dc.html` captured at 1440x900 into `/private/tmp/pos-handoff-sources/home/` (page, the asset drawer for the house and for the truck, the warranty drawer, the Log service drawer, the Vehicles filter, a later month selected, dark and light; plus a job marked done and an empty month in dark; 16 PNGs). The app already has the right bones and the right rules: the same four KPIs, the kind filter, an asset list, a twelve-month calendar with a selected month and its jobs, warranties sorted by expiry, a Needs attention rail with Mark done and Snooze 30d, the Property and Vendors cards, an asset drawer with facts and service history, a warranty drawer, a Log service drawer, and every drawer, filter and month in the URL. What differs is nearly all chrome: the band writes the summary as the crumb (the artboard's crumb is the module and the "{n} jobs due this month · ${cost} estimated" line with its dot sits on the right, the Insurance pattern); the lede is two sentences (the artboard's is one); "Log service" is a button inside the attention card (the artboard's is the page's primary, an accent 40px button in the header); the KPIs are the shared joined strip with tracked labels (the artboard draws four separate cards with 10px gaps, 11px sentence-case names and a 10px coloured note, and compacts the money to "$743k" and "$21.3k"); assets are list rows with three chips (the artboard draws a 2-up card grid: kind and state pill, 17px name, VALUE and ANNUAL COST cells, a next-job footer, amber border when service is due); the month cells are a 12-across joined grid (the artboard's are separate 110px cards with a count, up to four coloured dots and the cost, wrapping 6 by 2); the month's jobs have no status dot; warranties have a chip and a date (the artboard's is a table with a COVER · EXPIRES · FILE header and three mono cells); the attention rows are list rows with chips (the artboard's are bordered cards, red when overdue, with OVERDUE / DUE NOW and THIS MONTH heads); the Property card prints the asset's facts (the artboard's prints mortgage, rate, escrow, tax, insurance and equity); vendors are rows with a date on the right (the artboard's are three-line rows with the trade as a mono tag). The drawers are close (rows, SERVICE HISTORY, the same six form fields) except the artboard's form has a Notes field and a hint line under the pills, and its footer has a Close or Save service accent button. The app has three things the artboard lacks: a Due soon and a Snoozed chip on an asset (the artboard's card knows only SERVICE DUE and GOOD), snoozed jobs kept on the calendar (the artboard drops them), and the "It comes back, it does not go away" snooze toast. The artboard has five things the app cannot honestly draw as written: the Property card's mortgage, rate, escrow and equity rows (no source anywhere: Finance's account kinds are checking, savings, brokerage, retirement, credit, crypto and other, so a mortgage is a Finance migration), the warranty FILE cell's page counts (no PDF parser), an Undo on a marked-done job (marking done writes a `service_log` row and moves the schedule, per the README), "Cost posted to Finance under Home" in the one-off save toast (nothing posts to Finance), and an "Other" vendor option (nothing to store it in).

Decisions with Nick (append to `decisions/log.md`):
- Property card: Mortgage balance, Rate · term, Escrow and Equity have no source (Finance has no loan or mortgage account kind; adding one is a Finance migration, not this pass). Default: left out. The card draws the property's `facts` rows in the artboard's row shape plus one "Insurance" row read from `insurance.property_premium` (the metric Insurance already exposes "for Home's property card"; Home reads `finance.net_worth` today and prints nothing from it, so that read goes). Footer: "Coverage comes from Insurance." with the link; "Coverage lives in Insurance, which is not installed." when the metric is null. The mortgage clause is not written.
- KPI notes: "House at 92% of it" is computable (property value over combined value). The README says the total is labelled "your own estimate", and "Tax and insurance included" is a claim about what the owner typed into `annual_cost_cents`, which the app cannot know. Default: Combined value note "House at 92% · your own estimate"; Annual upkeep note "Your own figures". Alternative: the artboard's notes as drawn.
- Warranty FILE cell: `document_url` is a plain URL, there is no PDF parser and no size. Default: "PDF" when a URL is set (opens it), "NO FILE" otherwise; page counts left out (the Insurance decision).
- Asset card state pill: the artboard knows SERVICE DUE (amber) and GOOD (accent) only. Default: OVERDUE in red when the asset's soonest job is overdue (the app knows it, and the attention card already draws OVERDUE red), SERVICE DUE when due this month, GOOD otherwise; the app's Due soon and Snoozed chips go (both read GOOD, as the artboard does).
- Undo: the artboard's done state is session state. In the app Mark done writes history and rolls the schedule (README). Default: no Undo and no struck row; the job leaves this month and lands on its next interval; toast "Marked done. It moves to the service history and reschedules on its interval." (artboard copy, true).
- Snoozed jobs: the artboard drops a snoozed job from the calendar; the app's digest counts its cost ("the snooze hides the nag, not the bill"). Default: a snoozed job stays in its month with an `--ink-4` dot and "snoozed until {date}" in its meta, and leaves Needs attention, as today. The snooze toast keeps the app's "Snoozed 30 days. It comes back, it does not go away." (the README's rule, asserted by e2e) over the artboard's "Snoozed 30 days.".
- Not written because untrue or sourceless: "Cost posted to Finance under Home" (the one-off save toast reads "Logged to the service history."), the "Other" vendor option, the header's theme button (the sidebar's, per the shell decision). The form's vendor default is "Did it myself" (the app's; the artboard preselects a vendor, which would claim one).
- The drawer: the artboard's is 460px with a 22px title and a "Close esc" text button. Default: the shared Overlay at `narrow` (480px) with its band and 26px title, not re-derived; the artboard's footer buttons go in the footer slot.
- Title: "Home & assets" (the artboard; the sidebar already says "Home & Assets") replaces "Home and assets"; the search placeholder follows.
